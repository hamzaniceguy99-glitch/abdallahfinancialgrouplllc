Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Does my homeowners policy cover flood?

Almost certainly not. Flood is excluded from standard homeowners cover and needs a separate policy. It is the single most common and most expensive misunderstanding in Florida insurance.

Am I covered now that you have quoted me?

No. A quote is an estimate subject to underwriting. Cover exists only when the carrier confirms it in writing, and we cannot bind it by phone or email.

Can you advise on my 401(k) or investments?

No. That is securities and investment advice, which requires separate registration we do not hold. We will refer you to someone appropriately registered rather than offering an opinion.

How are you paid?

By commission from the carrier whose policy you buy, and we will tell you that plainly. Any separate fee is disclosed in writing before it is charged.

Is the cheapest health plan the best value?

Rarely. A low premium with a high deductible and a narrow network often costs more overall, and your doctors may be out of network. We compare on likely total cost instead.

Can you help if I have a claim?

We will help you report it and chase it, at no extra charge. The coverage decision is the carrier's, not ours, and we will tell you honestly when a denial looks correct.
